Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical lead time?

Lead times vary based on project complexity. Simple jobs can be completed in 2-3 days, while complex assemblies may take 2-4 weeks. We always provide a specific timeline with your quotation.

Do you accept small orders?

Yes! We serve customers from single prototypes to high-volume production runs. Our minimum order value is ₹5,000 for most services.

Can you handle custom designs?

Absolutely. Our engineering team can review your CAD files, suggest design optimizations for manufacturability, and execute prototypes for validation.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept: PDF, DWG, STEP, IGES, DXF, STP, and image files (JPG, PNG). If you have files in other formats, please contact us.

Do you offer assembly services?

Yes, we provide assembly services for components we manufacture or source. This includes quality verification and testing.

What about quality assurance?

All products undergo rigorous QA/QC testing using CMM machines, surface finish testers, and dimensional verification tools. We maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification.
