About Skanda Engineering

20+ years of excellence in precision manufacturing

Our Story

Founded in 2004, Skanda Engineering has established itself as a leading precision manufacturing company in Bangalore. With over two decades of experience, we have successfully served 200+ clients across automotive, aerospace, industrial machinery, and electronics sectors.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred manufacturing partner for OEMs and component suppliers. We invest continuously in technology and talent to deliver world-class manufacturing solutions.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class precision manufacturing solutions with exceptional quality, competitive pricing, and unmatched customer service. We aim to be the trusted manufacturing partner for innovative industries.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized precision manufacturing hub. We envision a future where Skanda Engineering leads innovation in manufacturing technology and sets industry benchmarks for quality and sustainability.
